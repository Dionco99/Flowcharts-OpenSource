    How these numbers are defined
    1R
    The account currency lost if the stop fills, including the spread and the round-turn commission. Lot size is floored to the lot step, so the trade risks that amount or less.
    Average R
    The mean realized R across closed trades. A filled stop is −1R.
    Expectancy
    Win rate times average winning R, minus loss rate times average losing R. The card also shows the mean currency result per trade.
    Profit factor
    Gross winning currency divided by gross losing currency.
    Max drawdown
    The largest peak-to-trough drop on the equity curve, in currency and as a share of the peak.
    Streaks
    The longest run of winning closes and of losing closes. A scratch resets both.
    RR achieved and planned
    Planned RR is the average of target distance over stop distance. Achieved RR is average winning R divided by average losing R.
    Session, weekday, setup
    Currency P&L grouped by the trade's session label, its UTC weekday, and its setup label.
